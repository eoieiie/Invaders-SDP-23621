# A1

## Team Introduction

Our team expands the fleet of Space Invaders. The baseline game ships a single player vessel and three visually distinct but behaviourally identical enemies. We introduce selectable player ships with meaningfully different play styles and enemy types with distinct behaviour, so that every level feels different and the player has a real tactical choice before the game starts.

We treat this as an extensibility problem first and a content problem second: our goal is that any future team can add a new ship type by writing one class and one sprite entry, without touching the game loop.

## Team Requirements

Player & Enemy Ship Variety. Replace the single fixed player ship with a set of selectable ships that differ in movement speed, firepower and durability, and extend the enemy roster with types that differ in behaviour rather than only in appearance. Both sides of the change are built on a common, data-driven ship abstraction so that the roster can grow without further changes to the game loop.

## Detailed Requirements

### Independent Requirements (DR-1–DR-8)

The following eight requirements can be implemented and verified by Team A1 independently. Integration with other teams is described separately below.

### DR-1 · Ship Abstraction

Refactor `Ship` into an abstract base type whose subtypes define movement speed, shooting interval, bullet speed, simultaneous bullet count, and maximum health. The Standard ship must reproduce the baseline gameplay exactly.

**Verification:** Update all existing `new Ship(` call sites, including `DrawManager.drawLives` and `GameScreen`, so they compile and function correctly after the refactoring.

### DR-2 · At Least Four Player Ship Types

Provide at least four player ships with distinct stat profiles, such as Standard, Speeder, Tank, and Sniper.

**Verification:** Each ship's measured movement speed and shooting interval must match its configured values.

### DR-3 · Player Ship Health Model

Replace the current single-hit life-loss rule with a per-ship maximum health model, allowing ships such as Tank to survive two hits. A life is lost only when the ship's health reaches zero.

**Verification:** Starting with three lives and full health, Tank must retain all three lives after two hits and lose one life on the third hit, leaving two lives.

### DR-4 · Ship Selection Screen

Insert a ship selection screen into the game start flow. Players cycle through ships using the left and right arrow keys, view the current ship's sprite and stats, and confirm their selection with Space. Pass the confirmed ship to `GameScreen` so that the selected ship is instantiated for gameplay.

**Verification:** The ship selected on the selection screen must be the ship used during gameplay.

### DR-5 · Ship-Specific Sprites

Give each player ship its own idle and destruction sprites. Append new entries only after existing entries in both the `SpriteType` enum and `res/graphics`.

**Verification:** All existing sprites must retain their correct mappings and appearance. This is a required regression check because the parser depends on entry order.

### DR-6 · At Least Three New Enemy Types

Add at least three enemy types with behaviours distinct from the existing A/B/C types: Shielded (destroyed after two hits), Swift (1.5 times the baseline horizontal movement speed), and Splitter (splits into two smaller enemies when destroyed). Each new type must have its own point value.

### DR-7 · Enemy Health and Damage Feedback

Allow `EnemyShip` to support health requiring multiple hits to deplete, and change its sprite to reflect its remaining health.

**Verification:** Shielded must change its sprite after the first hit and be destroyed on the second hit.

### DR-8 · Configurable Enemy Formation

Allow `EnemyShipFormation` to generate formations from an enemy type composition specification. When no specification is provided, it must reproduce the original A/B/C layout exactly.

Team A1 implements the formation builder independently. Supplying level-specific composition values is an integration task with the **6. Level Design System** team below.
